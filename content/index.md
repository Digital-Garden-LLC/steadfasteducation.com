# Special education management, all in one place

Infinity by Steadfast Education helps districts plan and manage Early On, Special Education, C4S, and 504 students in a single system, with Medicaid billing and state reporting built in.

[Contact us](mailto:help@steadfasteducation.com)

## Everything your team needs in one system

- **All-in-One Management:** Plan and manage Early On, Special Education, C4S, and 504 students in a single system.
- **Smart Tracking:** View due dates, missing documentation, and task status at a glance.
- **504 Plan Management:** Create, update, and maintain compliant 504 Plans with ease.
- **Integrated State Reporting:** Simplify data collection and submission for state requirements.
- **Medicaid Billing:** Complete billing directly within the same system—no duplicate entry.
- **Custom Reporting:** Generate tailored reports to monitor progress and ensure compliance.

## Why Infinity?

Empower your district with a smarter, faster way to manage student plans and performance.

- **Save Time:** Streamline planning, tracking, and reporting to focus more on student success.
- **Full Medicaid Integration:** Automate billing and compliance with a secure, all-in-one Medicaid system.
- **Seamless Collaboration:** Work together across teams on a web-based IFSP platform.
- **Electronic Signatures:** Simplify approvals within the IFSP and Plan of Care (POC for C4S, 504 plan) processes.
- **Smart IEP Guidance:** Follow step-by-step workflows designed by educators to improve accuracy and efficiency.

Infinity was built in partnership with educators and districts to save time, reduce costs, and enhance outcomes for every student.

## The Benefits of Infinity

### Simple processes ensure speed and compliance

- Be notified of IEPs and REEDs coming due, manage Medicaid encounters, and document Part C requirements such as IFSPs and Transitions coming due
- Easily track and report student data, evaluations, encounters, scheduling, SPP indicators, and all state and federal regulatory requirements

### Quickly plan and collaborate on a student's performance

- Confidently meet compliance requirements under the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA)
- One single and secure web-based system allows multiple users to access IEPs while providing staff with up-to-date information

### Simply manage every aspect of Medicaid

- Streamline your electronic claim processes with automatic management of encounter entry, tracking, billing, and more.
- Match all encounters to the contents of the student’s prescribed IEP and prescription information, assuring IEP compliance in terms of Medicaid billable services.

### Easily Share Links and Resources With Staff

- Notify Users of information related to your district and more
- Set up links to resources for staff
